FIRST SCHEDULE

States and Union Territories

The First Schedule specifies the name and territory of the States and Union Territories in accordance with Article 1 and Article 4 of the Constitution.
ABOUT THE FIRST SCHEDULE

The First Schedule lists all the States and Union Territories of India and describes the extent of their territories.

Parliament can change the areas, boundaries or names of any State or Union Territory by law as provided in Article 3.

WHAT DOES THE FIRST SCHEDULE CONTAIN?
  • The name of each State and Union Territory.
  • The extent of the territory of each.
  • Boundaries as they existed when the Constitution came into force (26th January, 1950) and as changed from time to time.

IMPORTANT POINTS
  • The number of States and Union Territories may change with time by the Parliament.
  • New States can be formed, boundaries can be altered or names can be changed under Article 3.
  • The First Schedule is not rigid; it can be amended by law.
EXAM NOTE
The First Schedule forms the basis for the political map of India as recognized by the Constitution.
