ABOUT SCHEDULES

The Constitution originally contained 8 Schedules. Through various amendments, 4 more were added, bringing the total to 12. They cover a wide range of subjects including states and union territories, emoluments, oaths, allocation of seats, tribal areas, official languages, land reforms, anti-defection provisions, panchayats, and municipalities.
