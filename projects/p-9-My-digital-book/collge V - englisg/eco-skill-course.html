Unit I

Data Collection and Sampling Techniques

1.1 Primary vs. Secondary Data
Parameter Primary Data Secondary Data
Origin First-hand data collected by investigator for specific purpose. Second-hand data compiled from existing publications.
Collection Methods Questionnaires, interviews, direct observation, surveys. Government reports (NSSO, Census, RBI bulletins), research journals.
Cost & Time High cost, labor intensive, time consuming. Cost effective and immediately accessible.
1.2 Sampling Methods: Probability vs. Non-Probability
Probability Sampling Methods:
  • Simple Random Sampling: Every population item has an equal, non-zero selection chance (Lottery method, Random number table).
  • Stratified Random Sampling: Population divided into non-overlapping strata; samples drawn proportionally from each stratum.
  • Systematic Sampling: Selecting every $k$-th item after a random start ($k = N/n$).
Unit IV

Index Numbers and Statistical Report Writing

4.1 Fisher's Ideal Index & Reversal Tests
Fisher's Ideal Price Index Formula:
$$P_{01}^F = \sqrt{L \times P} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum p_1 q_0}{\sum p_0 q_0} \times \frac{\sum p_1 q_1}{\sum p_0 q_1}} \times 100$$

Reversal Tests Verification:

1. Time Reversal Test: $P_{01} \times P_{10} = 1$
2. Factor Reversal Test: $P_{01} \times Q_{01} = \frac{\sum p_1 q_1}{\sum p_0 q_0} = \text{True Value Ratio}$.
Fisher's index satisfies both tests and is therefore termed the 'Ideal' Index.

Practice MCQs
Q1. Which index number satisfies both Time Reversal and Factor Reversal Tests?
A) Laspeyres Index
B) Fisher's Ideal Index
C) Paasche Index
D) Marshall-Edgeworth Index
Explanation: Fisher's index is the geometric mean of Laspeyres and Paasche and satisfies both fundamental reversal tests.
