Unit II

Digital Surveying: KoboToolbox, ODK & GPS

2.1 Digital Mobile Surveys using KoboToolbox & ODK

Modern fieldwork replaces paper forms with the KoboCollect App on Android smartphones to capture offline household data, geographic coordinates (Latitude/Longitude), and photo evidence.

KoboToolbox Survey Workflow:
  1. Create a research account on kobotoolbox.org.
  2. Design questionnaires via XLSForm or Drag-and-Drop builder (Include mandatory GPS Point & Image fields).
  3. Download form to mobile devices running KoboCollect.
  4. Conduct offline household surveys in remote areas and sync data once internet connectivity is restored.
  5. Export data as Excel, GeoJSON, or CSV files for spatial analysis in QGIS.

Practical Viva Voce Question Bank
Viva Q1 What does R.F. stand for on a Toposheet?

Answer: Representative Fraction. For example, an R.F. of 1:50,000 means 1 cm on the map represents 50,000 cm (or 500 meters) on the ground.

Viva Q2 What is GPS and how does it determine location?

Answer: Global Positioning System. It calculates exact latitude, longitude, and elevation by triangulating signals from at least 4 satellites.

Viva Q3 Which bearing system is read using a Prismatic Compass?

Answer: Whole Circle Bearing (WCB), measured clockwise from magnetic north from 0° to 360°.
