OUR STORY & ETHOS

From Our Green Meadows To Your Breakfast Table

Milk & Meadow started in 2011 with a simple realization: families in our cities were drinking over-processed, reconstituted milk that lost its natural soul and enzymes weeks before reaching stores.

We revived the ancient Indian tradition of caring for healthy, happy cows grazing freely on organic green fodder. By combining time-tested Bilona churning with modern cold-chain logistics, we ensure the milk your children drink is as pure as nature intended.
