OUR PHILOSOPHY

Street Food. Done Better.

Roll House began with a single mission: to rescue the legendary Kolkata Kathi Roll from greasy roadside stalls and craft it with premium ingredients, hygienic kitchens, and uncompromising passion.

We slow-marinate our meats in hand-ground masalas, use 100% whole malai paneer, and roll our layered laccha parathas fresh for every ticket. No shortcuts. Just pure, unadulterated street joy.
