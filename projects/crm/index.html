📖 How to Use — Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these 10 practical steps to manage your property inventory and daily operations smoothly
1

Start from Dashboard

Begin on the centralized Dashboard for an instant high-level overview of total properties, available inventory, upcoming projects, and pending follow-ups.

2

Add Property in Properties

Navigate to the Properties section and click ＋ Add Property to create a new residential or commercial inventory record.

3

Save Complete Details & Photos

Fill in and save comprehensive details including title, type, location, BHK, dimensions, pricing, photos, owner/contact details, and internal notes.

4

Find via Search & Filters

Use search and multi-filters (Location, Type, BHK, Price Category, Budget Slab, Status) or Master Search to quickly find matching listings for client inquiries.

5

Save to Favorites

Manage Upcoming Projects

Go to the Upcoming section to record and track developer projects, builder launches, brochures, configurations, and expected possession schedules.

7

Track Follow-ups

Use the Follow Up section to track critical buyer calls, site inspection schedules, and seller negotiations with automatic urgency status badges.

8

Review Full Property Details

Click any property card or table row to open the complete detail view, inspect high-resolution photos, review dimensions, and access owner contact numbers.

9

Update Records as Info Changes

Keep your inventory accurate and trustworthy by updating pricing, modifying status badges (Available, Hold, Sold), or adjusting notes as deals progress.

10

Use Theme & Settings Options

Switch between Light and Dark themes to suit your working environment, and customize categories, locations, price slabs, and filters in Settings.

🧩 Core Modules & Section Guide

How each section of the application operates
📊

Dashboard

Your central command center showing inventory health and scheduled activities:

  • Real-time Metrics: Live counter cards for Total Properties, Available, On-Hold, Upcoming Projects, Favorites, and Follow-ups.
  • Primary Quick Actions: Rapid ＋ Add Property, ＋ Add Project, and 🔍 Master Search buttons.
  • Needs Your Attention: Proactive alert feed displaying client follow-ups due today, upcoming calls, and properties under active negotiation.
  • Inventory Distribution: Progress bars illustrating property concentration across your registered locations.
🏘️

Properties

Complete property inventory management with advanced multi-filter controls:

  • Add & Edit Listings: Capture Name, Location, Type, BHK, Area (sqyds), Dimensions, Facing, Road Width, Price (₹), Status, Owner/Broker contact, Follow-up date, Map link, Video URL, and Notes.
  • Auto-Determined Badges: Dynamically calculates Price Categories (Below ₹1 Cr, Economical, Mid-Premium, Luxury) and Budget Slabs.
  • Photo Support: Choose local JPG/PNG photos (saved in IndexedDB) or paste external photo URLs with full lightbox viewer.
  • Dual View Modes: Switch between structured ☷ Table view and visual ▦ Cards view.
  • Multi-Criteria Filtering: Filter by Location, Type, BHK, Price Category, Budget Slab, and Status with multi-option sorting.
🏗️

Upcoming Projects

Dedicated pipeline tracking for developer projects, builder launches, and future developments:

  • Project Records: Project Name, Location, Developer / Builder, Project Type, Configuration, Starting and Maximum Price, Expected Launch, Expected Possession, and Status (Upcoming, Pre-Launch, Launched, Ready).
  • Independent Project Filters: Dedicated filter bar for project locations, types, price categories, budget slabs, and launch statuses.
  • Brochures & Elevations: Upload project renders or floor plans to showcase upcoming developments to buyers.
🔍

Master Search

Unified global search engine accessible via the bottom navigation Search button:

  • App-Wide Search: Searches across properties, projects, locations, localities, cities, owners, developers, phone numbers, and notes simultaneously.
  • Clear Source Badges: Every search result card clearly highlights whether the record is a 🏠 PROPERTY or 🏗️ PROJECT.
  • Multi-Token Search: Type multiple keywords (e.g. "Duplex Rajpur" or "Villa Jakhan") to filter records accurately.
  • Unified Filtering: Combine freeform search queries with Type, Location, Price Category, and Status dropdowns.
⭐

Favorites

Quick shortlisting for high-interest properties and client recommendations:

  • One-Click Bookmark: Tap the star icon (⭐) on any property or project card/detail modal to bookmark immediately.
  • Combined Shortlist: Open Favorites to view all shortlisted properties and upcoming projects side-by-side.
📅

Follow-ups

Stay on top of scheduled client calls, buyer site visits, and seller negotiations:

  • Scheduled Dates: Assign a follow-up date when creating or editing any property record.
  • Categorized Statuses: Automatically groups follow-ups into Overdue, Due Today, and Upcoming.
  • Direct Communication: Click telephone links directly to initiate calls or messages to property owners and buyers.
👤

Profile

Personal profile branding and live agent activity metrics:

  • Agent Details: Manage Full Name, Role / Designation (e.g. Property Manager, Broker, Sales Director), Phone, Email, and Company.
  • Profile Photo: Upload a custom profile picture with instant preview across headers and avatar badges.
  • Live Summary: Shows real-time counts of managed properties, upcoming projects, and scheduled follow-ups.
CENTRAL CONFIGURATION SYSTEM

⚙️ Settings → Categories & Filters

The single source of truth for all dropdowns, types, locations, ranges, and filters
💡
CRITICAL PRINCIPLE: Settings is the ONLY place where categories & filters are managed!

Normal application pages (Properties, Upcoming, Forms, Search) are strictly for USING options in clean dropdowns. When you want to add, edit, reorder, or disable a category or filter, do it exclusively inside Settings → Categories & Filters. Changes automatically become available in the relevant dropdowns and filters throughout the app!

What you can configure in Settings:

🏘️ Types & Sub-categories

Property & project types (Villa, Duplex, Apartment, Plot, Commercial). Add sub-categories as comma-separated tags (e.g. Independent Villa, Luxury Penthouse).

📍 Locations

Prime areas, localities, and cities (e.g. Rajpur, Jakhan, Sahastradhara Road). Autocompletes location inputs across property and project forms.

🏷️ Statuses

Inventory lifecycle states (Available, Hold, Sold, Pre-Launch, Ready) with customizable badge color themes (Green, Amber, Red, Purple).

💰 Price Categories

Define price brackets (Below ₹1 Cr, Economical, Mid-Premium, Luxury) with exact minimum and maximum amounts in ₹.

📊 Budget Slabs

Configure budget slabs (₹1 Cr, ₹2 Cr, ₹5 Cr+) used for rapid financial filtering during client inquiries.

🔃 Sort Options

Configure sort behaviors (Newest First, Oldest First, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low, Name: A to Z).

⚡ Custom Filters

Custom dimensions such as Facing (East, North, West, South), BHK Configuration, Road Width (20 ft, 30 ft), and Furnishing.

🎯 "Applies To" Scoping

Each entry can be set to 🌐 Both, 🏠 Property Only, or 🏗️ Project Only. A project-only type will appear in Project forms & filters, but never clutter property dropdowns.

🔃 Reorder & Enable / Disable

Use [ ▲ ] and [ ▼ ] to reorder entries in dropdowns. Use the Active / Disabled toggle switch to temporarily hide seasonal or discontinued options without deleting data.

🏷️ "Example" Sample Badges

The app comes with 5 demo entries in each category marked "Example — You can edit or delete this". Editing an example or creating a new entry turns it into normal user data and removes the badge.

💾 Backup, Import & Data Safety

Safeguarding and transferring your inventory database
⬇️
⬇ Backup (Export to Excel .xlsx)

Click the ⬇ Backup button in the top header to download your entire database into an Excel spreadsheet (.xlsx), complete with photos, specs, notes, and category configuration.

⬆️
⬆ Import (Restore Data)

Click ⬆ Import to upload an Excel (.xlsx) or JSON backup file. This allows you to migrate data between computers or restore your inventory if you switch browsers.

🌓 Light & Dark Themes

Comfortable viewing in any lighting environment

Property Manager Pro supports both high-contrast Light Mode and glare-free Dark Mode.

To toggle themes on desktop or mobile, open the bottom menu (☰) and click Theme (🌓). Your preference is preserved across browser sessions.
