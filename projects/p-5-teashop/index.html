Our Story

More Than Just Tea — A Space to Reconnect

Founded with a deep reverence for the timeless Indian tapri culture combined with the tranquil elegance of a modern tea lounge, CHAI & LEAF is an urban sanctuary.

We partner directly with family-owned tea estates in Assam, Darjeeling, and the Nilgiris to bring you unadulterated single-origin leaves. Whether you’re meeting friends, reading a book, or pausing midway through a hectic workday, every pour is crafted to deliver comfort and serenity.
